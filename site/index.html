Alder is free software for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV. Keep your Stremio setup on your phone or computer; Alder takes it from there on the TV.

Get the latest APK Read the full setup guide ↗
  1. 1

    Set up Stremio

    Sign in on your phone or computer and install your debrid stream add-ons.

  2. 2

    Install Alder

    Download Alder.apk from the latest release and install it on your TV.

  3. 3

    Sign in on your TV

    Open Alder and enter the code on screen in Stremio.

Requires Android 6.0 or newer, a Stremio account with debrid stream add-ons, and a way to install an APK.
