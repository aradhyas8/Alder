THE ALDER EXPERIENCE

The film is the main thing.

Alder keeps the screen clear and the remote simple. Your Stremio account, add-ons, library, and progress come with you.

01 / START

One clear way in.

Open a title and press Play. Alder finds the best source for your TV and starts it directly.

Hold OK when you want to choose a source yourself.
02 / CHOOSE

Options, when you want them.

Sources appear in plain columns, with the best match marked. Dead links give way to the next source from the same moment.

03 / SETTLE IN

Just the controls you need.

Find audio and subtitles in one calm panel. Alder remembers the tracks you choose for next time.

MADE FOR A SHARED SCREEN

BRING ALDER HOME

A few steps to lights down.

Keep your Stremio setup on your phone or computer. Alder takes it from there on the TV.

Get the latest APK ↗

Android 6.0 or newer · A Stremio account with debrid stream add-ons · APK sideload access

  1. 01

    Set up Stremio

    Sign in on your phone or computer and install your debrid stream add-ons there.

  2. 02

    Install Alder

    Download Alder.apk from the latest release and install it on your TV.

  3. 03

    Sign in on your TV

    Open Alder and enter the code on screen in Stremio. Your library and progress appear automatically.
