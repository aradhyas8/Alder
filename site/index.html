Tonight's programme ↓

Tonight

From the sofa
to the film, quickly.

  1. 01

    Find something good

    Home shows one film at a time, full screen, with what you were watching right underneath.

  2. 02

    Press Play

    Alder picks a source your TV can play and starts it. If a link fails, it tries the next one from the same moment.

  3. 03

    Choose, if you want to

    Hold OK to see every source, with the best match on top and its quality and size beside it.

  4. 04

    Your sound, your subtitles

    Change audio or subtitles mid-film without losing your place. Then the controls step back.
